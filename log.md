**F53 (2026-11-04) Global against local application (Stage 4e, `run_local_vs_global.py`, `restore/local.py`).** Same method run (a) on the whole picture, (b) tile by tile on 2x2, 4x4, 8x8 grids with seamless blending, (c) once per region found from the picture (`dark3`: low/medium/high dark channel; `sharp2`: glass, blurred against sharp areas) and (d) once per region of the TRUE depth (`depth3`, synthetic haze and water only: an upper bound). 6 to 7 methods per medium; synthetic = calibrated presets, 8 scenes x 3 severities; real = 40 haze pairs and 58 raindrop pairs. Gains are paired per picture and severity (local minus global). Water has no real pairs.
- **Real haze: local helps.** Histogram equalisation 10.80 to 12.74 dB on 4x4 tiles (+1.95, better on 100% of pictures; doing nothing is 11.63, so local turns a harmful method into a helpful one); guided DCP 13.62 to 14.81 on 8x8 tiles (+1.19). Gray-world and unsharp unchanged.
- **Synthetic haze: local also helps, for the same methods.** Stretch +2.31 with true-depth regions (79% of pictures), hist_eq +0.96 with picture-found regions, guided DCP +1.11 on tiles. The picture-found regions (`dark3`) do worse than tiles for DCP, and for stretch the true-depth regions beat every other form, so depth information is the useful part.
- **Water (synthetic only): colour methods gain a little locally** (gray_world +0.78, von_kries +0.63, red_comp +0.39, udcp +1.24 on 8x8 tiles), but histogram and stretch lose (stretch -0.55 to -3.7). Not checked on real data.
- **Glass (raindrops): local is worse for global-statistics methods** (stretch -0.1 to -9.2, hist_eq -1.0 to -4.2) on both synthetic and real; each tile re-stretches its own range, and a tile with a drop gets a different stretch from its neighbours. The apparent gains of unsharp (+1.9 / +2.0 dB), flat_field (+2.4 real) and local_stats are not locality: those methods measure their scale as a share of the picture size, so on a tile the sharpening or film blur shrinks, the method acts more weakly, and the score moves towards doing nothing (24.48 dB on real, which still beats every variant of every method).
- **Caveats.** "Best local" in the printed summary is the maximum over five variants per method, so it is optimistic; the heat map shows all variants. Tiling changes the meaning of scale-relative methods (above). Picture-found regions use a crude cue (dark channel) and three regions; a better region finder might behave differently. PSNR against the clear picture penalises a local re-stretch that shifts absolute brightness.

**F59 (2026-11-04) How long should a chain be? Mathematical check (`analyse_chain_limits.py`) and test of chains of 4 and 5 steps (`run_longer_chains.py`).** Theory: haze, water, glass film and plastic are (blur then) a per-colour affine map, which composes into ONE affine map, so the damage is invertible in 1 to 2 steps; chains of whole-picture brightness steps are one function per colour, bounded by the oracle LUT; the true inverse is limited by 8-bit rounding times 1/t, predicted PSNR = -10 log10(mean(1/t^2) / (12 * 255^2)).
- **Held-out check, 18 pictures (none | 1 | 2 | 3 steps | oracle LUT | oracle affine | true inverse | predicted):** haze 11.86 | 12.58 | 13.50 | 13.52 | 19.79 | 15.00 | 30.89 | 18.70; water 15.36 | 17.82 | 18.00 | 17.96 | 22.48 | 19.88 | 46.11 | 45.85; plastic 16.01 | 18.61 | 16.94 | 17.23 | 24.84 | 24.40 | 30.82 | 54.33; glass 25.88 | 24.57 | 22.56 | 19.44 | 26.77 | 26.37 | (no inverse for raindrops).

- **Water: close, not better.** stretch_rgb > von_kries 16.81 against red_comp > von_kries > unsharp 17.40; stretch_rgb alone 14.82 against von_kries alone 17.17 (depth-dependent t, which a global range cannot follow). Real tank water: every chain with stretch_rgb scores 11.8 to 12.9 dB against 18.30 for doing nothing (the near-uniform picture gives the percentiles nothing to estimate; noise is amplified).
- **Glass:** flat_field chains 19.6 dB against 24.9 for stretch (raindrops are not a slow film).
- **Which had been tried before:** chains whose steps are all in the medium's earlier pool, at default settings, were searched (e.g. gray_world > dcp_guided, dcp_guided > gray_world, dcp_guided > gamma_auto for haze; for glass flat_field > stretch and stretch > flat_field); chains with stretch, stretch_rgb, the matched Wiener filter or (for haze outside the subtraction search) tarel > stretch had not. `report/final/tables/theory_chains.tex` lists each with a yes or no.
- **Best, typical and worst cases against the theoretical restoration** (figures `theory_<medium>.jpg`; best chain chosen per picture with the answer known, so optimistic): haze heavy -5.9 dB (the chain beats the true inverse, which fails from rounding when t<0.1 on 80% of pixels), medium 15.3 dB, mild 32.9 dB; water 13.9 / 30.3 / 37.8 dB; plastic -0.8 / 2.1 / 16.0 dB (the largest, a bright bathroom: the per-colour stretch probably maps the wrong range; not tested); glass (against the oracle LUT) 0.2 / 0.6 / 8.2 dB.

**F71 (2026-11-05) Why the gaps are so large, and which pictures are beyond repair (`analyse_beyond_repair.py`; tables `beyond_repair_*.csv`, figure `beyond_repair.jpg`).** Definition used: a synthetic picture is BEYOND REPAIR when more than half of its pixels have t < 0.0055 (the 8-bit rounding error 0.0011 multiplied by 1/t exceeds a typical scene contrast of 0.2, so the signal-to-noise ratio is below one); for real pictures, where t is unknown, the sharpness ratio (detail left relative to the clear picture) is used, and SSIM tells whether structure, not only brightness, is restored.
- **Synthetic (18 held-out pictures per row):** haze mild and medium and water mild, medium and heavy: 18 of 18 are estimation-limited (true inverse 10 dB or more above the best chain; no pixel below the limit; synthetic water's largest attenuation is exp(-4.8) = 0.008). Heavy haze: mean 46% of pixels unrecoverable; 6 of 18 pictures beyond repair, 11 where the best chain is within 3 dB of the (failing) true inverse or above it, 1 in between.
- **Real haze (185 pairs):** sharpness ratio below 0.1 for 38 pairs, all Dense-Haze (38 of its 55); there the best method still gains 4.7 dB PSNR but SSIM only rises from 0.296 to 0.456 (against 0.645 to 0.795 above ratio 0.3): brightness and colour come back, structure only in part. O-HAZE, I-HAZE and NH-Haze have no pair below 0.1. Example: Dense-Haze_16, ratio 0.041, fusion_dcp +6.8 dB, result a blue wash with the vase not recovered.
- **Real tank water (120 pairs):** best-of-17 gain 0.45 / 0.68 / 0.26 dB (sharpness ratio below 0.1 / 0.1 to 0.3 / above 0.3); 72% / 54% / 86% of the pairs gain less than 0.5 dB; SSIM 0.665 to 0.670 for the 39 densest pairs; by turbidity band the mean best gain is 0.46 (mild), 0.62 (medium), 0.51 (heavy). Mild pairs have little to repair (21 dB unrepaired); the dense ones (e.g. Exp31 Cam2 S10) are white-outs.
- **Raindrops:** not beyond repair but beyond GLOBAL repair (covered patches are gone, the rest is fine; inpainting is outside the study).
- **Six causes of the mismatch between measured and theoretical scores, in order:** (1) the theory is given the true t(x), glow and blur, the method must guess them (ill-posed for t(x)); (2) priors fail (dark channel of clear pictures 0.12 to 0.21, a stretch assumes a full range); (3) information destroyed by rounding times 1/t or by noise; (4) the model is wrong for real pictures (F66); (5) PSNR punishes brightness and colour shifts; (6) incomplete functions and settings (plastic, F70).
